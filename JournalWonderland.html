Day 1 - MCDay 0 - Log 1

>Type: Digital-Program
>Medium: Video Game-"Minecraft"-Modification
>Characteristics: Hints of sentience. Lawbound. Only seen affecting saves. Largely unknown.
>Risk Factor: Minimal. May cause corruption in personal copy of the program.
>Codename: wonderland.jar

My name is Mister Capgras. I am an independant paranormal investigator currently investigating the digital anomaly known as the "Wonderland Mod" or "wonderland.jar". The anomaly was sighted on many different Minecraft modding sites and communities during a period of November 20th 2025 to January 1st 2026. Only a few days were between each sighting. No longer than a week. When it's paranormal behaviour was reported, many major communities were quick to take the download down from their hosts. However, the anomaly can still be found in smaller, less moderated sites and various underground servers. I have acquired a copy of this file for myself, and will upload it to my game installation.
Investigation officially begins upon the creation of the first world.

DETAILS OF INVESTIGATION:
- The anomaly has an effect on the computer's graphics driver that causes any screenshots or recordings to be corrupted.
- Taking advantage of the anomaly's Lawbound status. I figured it would be powerless against an in-game method of picturing.
- In order to avoid putting the anomaly's Lawbound status at jeopardy, I cannot use an intrusive mod for this.
- The Exposure mod will be used for capturing imagery. It adds an in-game camera item that renders photographs pixel by pixel with it's own code.
- The downsides of this; this mod is meant for immersion and not functionality. Usage will be limiting and will require in-game resources.
- I cannot use another mod in order to alter the recipes. The anomaly could exploit that.

Day 1 - MCDay 1 - Log 2

The creation of the world was uneventful. It had successfully generated the terrain without any noticable differences in load time. I set the gamemode as survival. Having cheats turned on might give the anomaly more power over my save. The first two hours or so went by with basic survival gameplay. Nearby where I spawned was a Pillager outpost. I had never looted one before. I usually play with mods that are either in older versions or add so much new content finding one becomes way too unlikely. I went in without preperation. My foolish decision did not go well. Almost dying in the first 5 minutes of gameplay. Despite that, I managed to get up into the outlook area. The chest was very dissapointing. I did not take a single item from it. After getting back down and narrowly avoiding death by a thousand arrows, I found a desert. I started exploring it in search of a temple. I found no temples, but I did find two villages close to eachother. First one had dissapointing loot and terrible road design. I simply skimmed over it and went my way. The second one had decent loot but more importantly a cave entrance right next to it. I set up a small base and went down to mine. It was expansive. Gathered a sizable amount of ores. Not long, I got enough resources to craft the camera and a single film for it. At this point I have been playing on this world for almost two hours. I almost forgot I was supposed to look out for phenomena. Fortunately, it would not be much further until my first encounter. As I was gathering redstone, I heard a noise behind me. At first I thought it was cave ambience, but then realized I did not recognize this sound effect. When I turned back I saw a humanoid character reaching out to me. Pitch black skin save for a pupil covering it's face.* It dissapeared the moment we met gaze. The anomaly was finally starting to show signs, and it went for a direct approach. Outisde of that however it was nothing major, so I carried on with collecting ores. I did up my guard, though. I placed two furnaces to smelt some raw ores as I ventured deeper into the cave. That was not for long however, as I noticed faint footsteps near me. It was coming from inside the cave walls. They got closer and closer and then, I heard blocks getting broken. Something was advancing towards my position. I panicked, started running the other way and broke the two furnaces. I went back up through a stream of water and rushed the way out of the cave. I went back into the village. Whatever it was, didn't seem to pursue me any longer. But, something was still afoot. There is a river next to the village. On one of the sides of that river, were two oak trees. One of them was missing. More concerningly though, where it went was not a mystery. Next to the now sole tree, was an upside-down cross. Made out of oak logs. It, or maybe they, wanted me to know they were aware of my presence here.
Since that moment, I have been encountering many other occurances. Minor and major alike. It feels as if everytime I open this program, a new construct welcomes me with malignant arms. Every new dawn, I see a figure standing in front of my window. Sometimes they speak in the chat. Especially one of them likes to text a lot. It has no username, and a Steve with a blank face is always present when a message by them appears. Pretty sure that is the one sending the texts, but I can never be truly certain. It likes to stalk me. Everywhere I go, I believe it watches me. Even if I cannot see it. It sometimes directly confronts me. Appearing in-front of me and simply observing. It speaks but, I never seen it respond to anything I say myself. It never 'attacked' me but I dare not appraoch it. It keeps it's distance and I would like to keep it that way. Others are not as passive as that nameless one is, unfortunetly. If I encounter an entity, nine times out of ten it will pursue me. It gets tiring more than anything at some point to tell the truth. They want to take me to their wonderland. I wonder what is so special about it.

* Despite the similarities, this entity was not the Sentinel. The pupil abided by Minecraft's 8x8 head-skin ratio.

Day 2 - MCDay ## - Log 3

FIRST EXPEDITION LOG #1

Today, I was taken into the wonderland. Until this day I slept through every night in order to avoid dangerous encounters with entities. That proved to be a mistake. When my character woke up, the game put me in a loading screen. One you would see when going between dimensions. Once it was done loading, I found myself standing on a thin wood bridge. On my sides, cobble shapes floating in the air surrounded me. It was concerningly quiet, not even ambient music would play. It was as if I stumbled upon a decrepit reality never meant to have life present. I checked the dimension's assigned name through the F3 menu, it read; Inconsistency. I did not plan on going on an expedition this early. I was yet to make a steady source of food and proper tools and armor. I also had many valuables on me, including the camera. Nevertheless, I was not going to advance my investigation by staying here. I ventured forward. Yet, no matter how far I went nothing changed here. It was only me, the cobblestone and the void beneath. That was until I found a sign. Ironically, the only thing written on it was the word "JUMP" over and over again. So I did. Fell to the void I have, which fortunately did not serve my untimely demise. I descended on an ever expanding land of grass. Despite the harsh landing I was not damaged. My immediate surroundings only had trees orderly put in a grid pattern. All looked the same. Once again, I checked the F3 menu. Wonderland...
And so, here I was. In the promised wonderland. Or atleast a representation of it. Derelict and almost apocalyptic. The horizon stretched out infinitely. Yet, I could not see past a few blocks because of my render distance. The grass was so lush, it seemed improbable in such a wasteland. I went ahead. The more I uncovered the fog, there were only trees to greet me. I was beginning to believe I would be trapped here indefinetely, but I decided to persevere for another 5 minutes. That was the right call to make. I came across a village farm field, sitting in the middle of the trees. It grew wheat, all fully developed and looking healthy. Curiously, however. The water compartment had no liquids, but instead a series of strange pure white blocks inside of it. They were unnaturally bright. I check the block ID with the menu. "endless_field_teleporter". I grit my courage and touched one of the blocks. There I found myself in the air. Descending rapidly once again, this time to a landscape of nothing but wheat. As far as the eye can see, it was just wheat, wheat and more wheat. Makes you realize how much Minecraft would benefit from anti-aliasing. I walked through the fields, and on the horizon appeared a windmill. I went inside, it was in a state of disrepair. Cobwebs had surrounded the walls and a few blocks were missing. There was a ladder connecting to a second floor. In it, only a chest. It was filled with wheat. I will be dreaming of bread and malt tonight; I thought to myself. I went out to see something stick out of the fog. Approaching it, a wood wall uncovered itself. Thick, tall and wide. It had a cosmetic pattern on it constructed from slabs and stairs. Carved into the wall. In the middle was a set of oak double-doors. To me, this looked like the part of a mansion. Ripped out of the main body. I searched around it, but found no thing worthy of attention. Onwards I continued. Eventually I came across yet another construct. A house, a complete one. Albeit, small. Inside was a bed, a stove, a crafting table and a single chest. Not even double. Only one item was inside. A book and quill; and in it, only one page. In it, only one sentence. "what is the point?". I reflected. On what the purpose of such a place might be. Nothing but a ginormous farmfield. I felt... isolated. It felt suffocatingly lonely. All the activity here was to tend to the endless yields of crop. Yet it also felt, peaceful. If this is all I ever do, no one will bother me. No one will ever have a reason to pick on me, or visit me, or talk to me. I wondered, if I was given the opportunity to live the rest of my life in such a mundane haven. Would I? Shortly after that small ponder, I took the book with me and headed to a small shack I noticed nearby the house. It was almost a one-to-one replica of a village house you would see before 1.14. However, what truly was interesting about this was the glowing pillar housed by it. It looked to be the same type of block as the teleporter that took me here. A small peek at the ID confirmed this. "wonderland_teleporter". This will transport me back to where I was. I once again braced myself, and went in. Descending. Landing. Trees. If roads can lead me back to the heart, then getting out of here alive will be more complicated than anticipated. I went forward, now with a newfound dread. It did not take long until I encountered another irregularity. A big patch of shadow covering the ground. I could not see it, but something sizeable was above me. I gathered dirt from the ground and began pillaring upwards. As I was ascending, I noticed a figure at the corner of my eye. I turned my head towards it. It was the nameless stalker, with the faceless steve skin. Staring right at me, muttering nothing. It watched me as I pillared up to the floating structure. Eventually, something entered my rendered distance in the sky. Brick blocks, lots of them. Whatever this was, it was a behemoth. Reaching it's base, I started digging into it. Break one above, place one below. I repeated this cycle for roughly 2 minutes. When I saw space and light on the ceiling, it was red and dim. A room largely hollow was inside. Connected to it were 3 dead ends. One had a chest at the end, two had black blocks that trapped all light. Inside the chest was nothing but a book and quill. It read as follows; "this is not real there is no meaning no answers" in a frantic manner. I put the book in my inventory and opened the menu. "bricways_teleporter". I touched the block. I found myself inside of a narrow room. Made out of bricks. I broke out, to find a labyrinth of neverending amounts of corridors and bricks. True to it's name. The walls were adorned with redstone torches to illuminate the realm. They were dim, but it was still largely visible. I ventured into the hallways. Corner upon corner upon gateway upon gateway upon passage upon passage. I started to notice a pattern amongst these dimensions. They all repeat. All, made to grind your brain with repetition. Crafted with the intention of isolating the mind. Your brain turns numb seeing the same patterns for chiliad. I could not imagine being forced to waste my entire existance here. Then the repetition broke. A chest. In the middle of an intersection. Inside was expectedly a book. It read; "He will hear your breathing unless you crouch or stay away. Don't touch his block.". An entity is present here. And the wording implies it is problematic enough that is should be avoided. From then on I stopped sprinting down the halls. I took my time and at all times kept my ears on alert for this entity. Not long after, in the middle of a room was another chest with a book. "They ripped his eyes out and sewed his eye sockets shut. The only thing he can see is his block." I did not like what this entailed. It need not be said that the actions described here cannot be replicated inside of Minecraft. Was this anomaly more dangerous than previously thought? Does it have a victim count? Am I in true danger by meddling with these? These questions ran through my head. This reminded me of the previous texts I found. The cryptic wording would make more sense if it was by a real person trapped in a digital nightmare like this. For now, however. There was very little I could do. So I went further. A while of wandering later, I saw a much brighter and white-bluish light emanating from a room behind a corner. I slowly approached the luminance. Turning the gate's corner, I saw a bright blue block. Reminiscent of glowstone. It was placed upon a brick pedestal. Next to the pedestal was yet another chest containing a book. "i am not real. this place is not real. this is the end. there is nothing. no exit. i am alone here. but atleast i have this block. my block.". I heed the former book's warnings and skipped the room without touching the block. Though, only a few rooms away is yet another chest. This one also on a pedestal. "take his crystal. enrage the blind one. it is the only way to open the gates trapping you here.". This is like a cheap story, I thought to myself. After considering and reconsidering my options, I went back to the room with the block. After a little hesitation, I broke it. Four "Crystal Shards" were dropped. Their texture was nigh identical to Amethyst Shards, save for a blue coloring. I waited in anticipation... Nothing. Not even a sound. I stepped to get out of the room. The moment I did, I heard an exchale behind my character. It was rasp, digital, painful. Short after, rapid footsteps. I began fleeing. It gave chase. Considerbly faster than my running speed. I took sharp corners. Tried to create blockade with the blocks at my disposal. It would break them one by one. Determined to catch up to me. Eventually, it went a wrong turn. And so I went quiet. Holding shift the entire time, hand off the mouse. Once I heard it far enough, I began to move. Then on, it was silent. I went through corridors, constantly tense. Around 10 minutes of directionless walking later, I came across a white pillar of light. Or more accurately, more teleporters. Without thinking I went in. Descending. Landing. Trees. Back to the wonderland. Now I knew, death was a possibility here. All the items I had on hand were at jeopardy. Fortunately, though. I was playing on simple survival. No matter the deaths, there would be no permanent losses to the investigation. If something goes wrong, I can fix it. Atleast, that is how it seems for now. I reassured myself with that and began going along a straight line. Though, the wonderland was not as barren and peaceful as it was previously. I noticed something watching me. It was trying to hide itself behind the trees, although not very well. It did not resemble any of the entities I witnessed before. It looked like a humanoid player skin, but it was lank, bloody, contorted. It's most defining feature was the presence of a jaw. It was clear the model was not meant to bend like that. It was watching at a distance, ready to pounce like an ambush animal. I made sure to abstain from getting close. Yet it followed. It pursued my steps thinking I was not aware of it's existance. Eventually I ran, and so it chased. It was not very fast, but it was able to keep up. An endurance predator. While frantically running, I found a structure to shelter in. A box made out of what looked like diamond blocks. It was labelled with a sign that reads "richyDUDE2001's house". The door was iron, so it would make for a durable sanctuary. I rushed in. It kept the entity at bay. It was not making attemtps at forcing it's way in. Sadly, it was not distancing itself away from here. No matter how long it passed, it would stick around. Waiting for my departure. The box was not empty. It contained an assortment of valuables, a black teleporter and a chest with yet another book in it. "Whoever trusts in his riches will fall.". I did not desire to face the entity. So, not having many choices I went into the teleporter, which was named "the_vault_teleporter". After the loading screen, I was met with said riches. This entire dimensions halls, walls, everything, was strictly made out of valuable ores. Upon this sight, I failed to heed the text's warnings. My virtual greed overtook and I began collecting as many diamonds, gold and iron as possible. In my defense, I was in short supply and required them to further my research more effectively. I had to get out of here as fast as I could, however. I was capturing imagery with the camera as I went through these dimensions. But now the film began to run dry. Only three frames were left. My food supply was similarly diminishing. I could not stay in the wonderland much longer. I had to find a way back to the overworld. This 'vault' had a labyrinthine layout similar to brickways. This one, however, had much narrower corridors. Navigation was difficult. It was also not uncommon to find dead-ends. I had to mine through the walls multiple times. As I was traveling a figure appeared before me. A player staring right at me. The moment I gazed upon it, it vanished. I went on guard. I could not afford to deal with yet another entity. I made haste to find the way out. I even broke my pickaxe and had to make a new one. I tried to turn the ore blocks into materials, but...
It all turned into rotten items. Dirt blocks, ripped eyes of spiders, rotten flesh of the undead. The blocks were fraudulent. I should have expected this, yet I did not. And now I have fallen for the trickery, I might be doomed. This had the possiblity of being the reason it was stalking me. It might have been angry at me. Mad, for daring to touch it's precious stones. I had to resort for a wooden one, and for a feeling of despair, doom. I went through the halls as quickly as I could. To find the way back home, or atleast to the heart. Then, it appeared right in front of me. Did not dissapear this time. No. Instead, it began to adorn armor. Armed itself with a sword. And went in for the kill. Rapidly it began sending messages to the chat. Telling me to "die" and "leave". As if I had control. Again and again it struck me. My health was depleting dangerously quickly. I panicked. I had to act quickly. I hit ESCape. Time stopped. The entity was unmoving. I was suspended in air. The anomaly's biggest weakness. It was still lawbound despite everything. No matter what, it has to abide by the rules of the game. This was why I settled for so many restrictions. So we were on the same layer. And it finally paid off. As the player I still had the ultimate say in the game. So, I took advantage of that. I pressed Quit and left the game for the day. I would plan on how to handle this tomorrow. For now, richy will stay in stasis with it's bloodlust.

At the mercy of my will.
